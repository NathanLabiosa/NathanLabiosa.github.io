This work presents a **layer-wise analysis of perturbation robustness**, showing that a layer's sensitivity to perturbation, its causal effect on model output, and its repairability are **distinct, dissociable properties** rather than a single robustness axis.

Key findings:
- Sensitivity, causality, and repair effects diverge systematically across network depth
- The dissociation has direct implications for how robustness interventions **scale** with model size
- Results motivate layer-targeted, rather than uniform, robustness interventions

Currently under review at **ARR**.
